## Announcement

Scala Studios has decided to cease operations in this area of Minecraft. Rather than leave the work behind closed doors, we are releasing our latest source code to the community, including our practice code and our bots.

A lot of people asked for this code, and for the bots in particular. Here it is.

Our Minecraft infrastructure has since moved to an entirely new codebase, built on Kubernetes deployments across AWS and Azure. The projects in this organization are no longer part of that stack, and we do not plan to develop them further.
